Mid Stream – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This app is a private publishing tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (via TikTok and Google sign-in), the app accesses basic profile information, uploads videos the owner has approved, and reads view counts and comments on the owner's own videos.

How data is stored

All data, including access tokens, is stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Comment text may be sent to an AI service to draft replies that the owner reviews before sending.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok or Google account settings. Deleting the app's data folder removes all stored data.
